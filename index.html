Taipei, Taiwan

The power of narrative is a fascinating aspect of human cognition.

This is what draws me to the misinformation study in collaboration with the Taiwan FactCheck Center. Earlier, I also used EEG to record what the brain does while it reads.

Lately, this curiosity extends to how people think with, trust, and get persuaded by AI.

When I’m not doing research, I’m probably at a film screening, crocheting, vibe-coding a side project, or working on my French.

    Methods follow-up on previous data. At a fast 350 ms SOA, word-locked analysis gave a reversed position effect; ridge-regularized time-expanded deconvolution with nested cross-validation recovered the expected N400 reduction at later word positions.
